# Patterson Companies — GitHub Organization

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for **Patterson Companies, Inc.** — a leading specialty distributor serving dental and animal health professionals across the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.

## About Patterson Companies

Founded in 1877, Patterson Companies has grown from a regional dental supply business into one of the largest specialty distributors in North America. Today, our two core business segments connect healthcare professionals with the products, equipment, software, and services they need to deliver exceptional care.

| Segment | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Patterson Dental** | Products, technology, and services for dental professionals |
| **Patterson Animal Health** | Veterinary supplies, equipment, and practice management solutions |

## Our Mission

> *To lead select specialty markets in creating value for our customers and growth for our company through our products, services, technology, and relationships, while providing personal and professional growth for our employees.*

## Our Values

- **Partnership** — We collaborate closely with customers, suppliers, and one another to achieve shared success.
- **Integrity** — We act honestly, ethically, and transparently in everything we do.
- **Innovation** — We embrace new technologies and continuously improve to deliver better outcomes.

## What You'll Find Here

This organization hosts repositories for:

- **Internal tooling & automation** — Developer productivity tools, CI/CD pipelines, and shared workflows
- **Integration libraries** — SDKs and APIs that connect Patterson systems with partner platforms
- **Data & analytics** — Pipelines, models, and dashboards that drive business intelligence
- **Infrastructure as code** — Cloud infrastructure definitions and configuration management
- **Developer resources** — Templates, documentation, and community health files
